Key System Features

Designed for Academic Excellence & Real Agricultural Utility

CBAM Attention Mechanism

Includes Channel Attention (Global Avg & Max Pooling + Shared MLP) and Spatial Attention (Channel-wise pooling + 7×7 Conv) to focus on subtle lesion boundaries.

Explainable AI (Grad-CAM)

Generates true gradient heatmaps highlighting exactly which leaf surface regions influenced the neural network's final classification decision.

Real AI Integrity & Verification

Zero fake or hard-coded predictions. All probabilities are calculated directly from softmax vector outputs with dynamic confidence evaluation.

Integrated Encyclopedia

Detailed knowledge base containing symptoms, underlying causes, chemical/organic management, and preventive agricultural measures.

SQLite & Chart.js Dashboard

Tracks historical predictions, healthy vs. diseased ratios, top recurring plant pathologies, and average confidence trends.

Responsive PWA Design

Optimized for Windows desktop, tablets, Android, and iOS devices with web app manifest and service worker shell caching.
